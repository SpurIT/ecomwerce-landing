# Ads

Ads carry the same three things as the landing page: the promise, the product card on orange, and the price sentence when there is room.

## Formats

- Feed: 1:1 at 1080 x 1080 and 4:5 at 1080 x 1350.
- Reels, Stories and TikTok: 9:16 at 1080 x 1920.
- Account avatar: the app tile. The platforms crop it to a circle; the glyph sits inside the circle.

## Safe zones

- 9:16: keep the headline, the lockup and the product card inside x 120 to 940 and y 270 to 1250. The top 14 % carries the account name, the bottom 35 % carries the caption and the call to action, and TikTok stacks its buttons on the right.
- 1:1 and 4:5: keep 70 px clear on every side.
- Published figures for these zones disagree between sources. Check each creative in Ads Manager's placement previews and against TikTok's In-Feed safe-zone template before launch.

## Layout

- `orange` field; the product card with the merchant's real product photo; one headline of seven words at most in Gabarito; the lockup on orange, small, at the top left.
- The platform adds its own call-to-action button. Do not draw a fake one, and do not draw platform UI.
- In video, the first frame is the finished page; then the build plays once, 1.2 to 2 seconds.

## Copy

- The landing page's rules apply: measured numbers only, no outcome promises, no invented reviews, no "winning product", no income claims.
- Never show fake notifications ("New order! $49"), fake reviews, star ratings or countdowns.
- Use the word Shopify only to say the app works with Shopify; no Shopify logo except the official App Store badge, unaltered.
