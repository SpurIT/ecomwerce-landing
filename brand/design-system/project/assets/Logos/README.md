# Logos

- `ecomwerce-icon.svg` - the app icon: orange tile #FF6A13, white window and title line, ink #1A1410 button. Square; the App Store rounds the corners.
- `ecomwerce-icon-1200.png` - the file to upload as the App Store and Dev Dashboard icon.
- `ecomwerce-icon-512.png`, `ecomwerce-icon-192.png`, `ecomwerce-icon-180.png` - web app icons; 180 is the Apple touch icon.
- `ecomwerce-favicon.svg`, `ecomwerce-favicon-32.png`, `ecomwerce-favicon-16.png` - the small version for 16 to 24 px, corners rounded.
- `ecomwerce-icon-mono.svg` - single-color version: ink tile, white parts. Print only.
- `ecomwerce-icon-construction.svg` - the 16-unit grid, the 75 px margin and the 750 to 900 px band.
- `ecomwerce-lockup.svg`, `ecomwerce-lockup.png` - tile and ink wordmark, for light grounds.
- `ecomwerce-lockup-white.svg` - tile and white wordmark, for ink and dark grounds.
- `ecomwerce-lockup-on-orange.svg` - glyph without tile and ink wordmark, for orange fields.
- `ecomwerce-mark-on-orange.svg` - the glyph alone, for orange fields only.
- `ecomwerce-wordmark.svg` (ink #1A1410), `ecomwerce-wordmark-white.svg` (white) - the word alone.
