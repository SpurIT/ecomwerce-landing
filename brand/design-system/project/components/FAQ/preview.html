Does it change my live store?

No. Ecomwerce prepares the page and shows you a preview. Your store changes only when you press Go live.

Do you add reviews?

Only real ones. Ecomwerce never invents reviews, ratings or counters.

What does it cost?

Free to install. Your first 2 product pages are free, then $3 per product page. The monthly cap is $60.

Which languages does it write in?

English for now.
