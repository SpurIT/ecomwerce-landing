The Ecomwerce mark, wordmark and lockups, as files from the Logos asset group.

- Use the files; never redraw the mark or retype the wordmark.
- Pick the version by ground: `ecomwerce-lockup.svg` on light, `ecomwerce-lockup-white.svg` on ink or dark, `ecomwerce-lockup-on-orange.svg` on orange.
- Below 24 px use `ecomwerce-favicon.svg`, the small version without the title line.
- Give it clear space of half the tile height. Minimum: icon 16 px, lockup 24 px tile height.
- The consumer provides the ground and the size; nothing else changes.
