# shopify app store badge

the official "Find it on the Shopify App Store" badges, unaltered, from https://shopify.dev/zip/shopify-app-store-badges.zip (svg, 166 x 44). they are Shopify's files, not ours: never recolor, crop, shadow, animate or border them; use is authorized by the Partner Program Agreement, Part A 5.2 (https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/launch/marketing/shopify-brand-assets, read 2026-11-10).

- badge-shopify-app-store-dark.svg - the black badge (preferred), for light grounds
- badge-shopify-app-store-light.svg - the white badge, for dark grounds
- at least 30px high, clear space of half its height, always linked to the app's listing
- read by the brand previews (brand/tools/build-brand.mjs) and the landing page (landing/); the landing design links them from tasks/landing/design/assets/
